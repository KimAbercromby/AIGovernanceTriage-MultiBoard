How it works

One front door, proportionate governance pathways

Every system enters the same front door and is captured once under one AIR-ID. The AGPI establishes governance priority, while the risk assessment separately classifies risk as Low, Medium, High or Critical. Governance pathway is a separate decision: genuinely low-risk, non-acting systems may use the light-touch pathway; non-agentic systems use the non-agentic governance pathway; and systems that can take actions or exercise delegated authority use the agentic governance pathway. A non-agentic system may still be High or Critical risk. Where a system can execute actions without human review of each individual action, the mandatory agentic trigger applies and the minimum effective risk tier is Critical. A governance relay carries the case across the existing forums, with configurable forum names, a decision-ready evidence plan feeding the Decision-Ready Paper (WCC-AIG-38), an Excel-ready Register row and a separate planned-gate export.

The light-touch route is the quick path: a low-risk system that cannot act, does not use personal or special-category data needing assessment, does not affect residents or decisions about them, is not statutory or enforcement, is not novel or resident-facing, and is not a significant new spend, is registered as Priority 4 with a single light Register row and stops there. Width stays at the door; depth arrives only when the risk earns it.

Authority stays with each forum. The assurance board carries continuity and issues one assurance opinion; it does not replace strategic, technical, commercial or release authority. Final accountability stays with the Service Owner, SRO or delegated authority.

    Retire or decommission an AI system

    Retirement is a governance gate in its own right, not a triage. This mode does not score AGPI or plan a deployment route.
