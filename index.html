How it works

One front door, proportionate governance pathways

Use the tool to draft triage and prospective routing prompts for one AI system. AGPI prioritises governance attention; it does not waive Equality Act 2010 section 149, Human Rights Act 1998 section 6, privacy, data-protection or other case-specific duties. Every tier requires rights, equality and privacy screening. A non-agentic system may still be High or Critical risk. Agent authority and permissions belong in AIG-AGT-04; a triage score grants none. Outputs are review handoffs, not live Register rows, current assurance state, Gate Plan / Event / Condition entries or formal decisions.

One system thread: AIG-INV-04 (AI Register) owns the permanent Council-issued AIR-ID and current system state. Carry that same AIR-ID into AIG-DEC-04 (Gate Log) plans, dated events and event-linked conditions, with formal decisions in AIG-DEC-03 or approved minutes. These are linked records with distinct owners, not disconnected journeys. Proposed controlled AIG-INV-05 is a descriptive relationship map only—not approved/adopted and not a decision, permission or approval source.

A proportionate pathway may reduce review depth, but never skips initial equality, human-rights, privacy and other relevant screening. Specialists and legal owners confirm case-specific duties; no tier is a waiver or approval.

Authority stays with each forum. The AI Assurance Board gives assurance opinions and recommendations; it advises and never decides. The AI Governance Lead keeps the continuity record, and each decision is made by the officer or forum with confirmed delegation. Final accountability stays with the Service Owner, SRO or delegated authority.

Step 3 of 4

Risk classification and mandatory escalation

Impact uses the highest of five dimensions. Inherent risk is likelihood multiplied by impact; residual risk applies the established control effectiveness factor. The governing tier is the highest of the risk tier, any mandatory trigger floor, the impact floor (a confirmed Impact 5 sets at least Medium) and, for a use that can act, the agency-tier minimum (AIG-DEC-01 Agentic pathway). Step 4 of 4

Multi-board governance route

Your proposed route through the decision gates, set by the governing tier. Keep one system identity throughout the forum journey.

How the gates are chosen

The route lists the AIG-DEC-01 Gate Map decision gates for this use, set by the governing tier: Gate 2 and Gate 6 are mandatory for every use that can act, Gate 4 applies wherever a procurement, new contract, licence change or contract variation is needed (N/A with the reason for an existing contract or licence, a free public tool or an in-house build) and Gate 5 from Medium. This proposed route prepares the next handoff; an authorised decision, its dated gate event and the Register's current position are linked through the same AIR-ID. No gate is passed by this triage.

    Agentic triage — for systems that can act

    Complete this where the system can take actions. Score the five agency dimensions, flag the multipliers and capabilities, and confirm containment. The agency tier and routing follow the profile, not a sum, and a high-consequence or hard-to-contain reading (a high reversed controllability score) can raise the tier.

    The five scores determine a proposed route. Record a short reason and evidence reference for each score so the assessment can be reviewed. Persistence, delegation and other multipliers are separate flags. A blank reference remains outstanding evidence, not a verified control.

    An action-capable system needs a proportionate AIG-ASS-11 agentic security review at any baseline risk tier. Record applicable ASI01–ASI10 controls, owner, actual state and dated test evidence in AIG-AGT-04; set monitoring thresholds in AIG-OPS-02 before live use. A triage result cannot approve a pilot or production use. Required controls for consequential production autonomy must be implemented and evidenced, or covered by an effective, time-bounded compensating control expressly accepted under Council delegation in AIG-DEC-03 and linked to the dated AIG-DEC-04 Gate Event.
