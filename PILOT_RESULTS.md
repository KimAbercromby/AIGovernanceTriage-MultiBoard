The 05 row is only a candidate; a Plan ID, assessment authority, approvals, current assurance state, gate events, and runtime evidence are not invented.
- The revised **04** and **38** Word documents are not blank fillable templates: they contain narrative, worked examples and proposed review material, without supported editable placeholders. The app rejects them for automatic filling and continues to offer clearly labelled review handoffs. A compatible, deliberately prepared editable Word template with explicit placeholders can be filled locally; this is not the same as claiming the revised originals were completed.

**Limit:** Exact headers do not make a draft CSV import-ready. Confirm the current controlled source version, permanent AIR-ID, field owners, evidence, legal applicability and formal decisions before entering anything into authoritative records. No change to a live Pages site is part of this pilot.
